I led a seven-student team working with two IBM experts to build a Next.js assistant for new university students. The product reached **67 peak concurrent users** during its final demonstration.

The system used IBM watsonx Assistant and a **retrieval-augmented generation** pipeline over a question-answer knowledge base. I implemented Milvus vector retrieval with a FLAT index and backend APIs for real-time grounded responses.
