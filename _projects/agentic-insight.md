AgenticInsight automates essential stages of visual analytics while preserving opportunities for people to specify intentions, preferences, and domain knowledge.

I helped build the **LangGraph and OpenAI API** pipeline, including iterative agent loops, sandboxed code execution, function calling, visualization generation, and knowledge extraction. The system ranked **Top 5 among approximately 20 international teams** and was presented orally at **IEEE VIS 2025**.
